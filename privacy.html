What this app does

tech-shorts (Learn & Implement) is a personal tool that creates short videos from a topic and, if the owner chooses, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Google / YouTube data we use

The app asks for one permission: upload videos to YouTube (scope youtube.upload). It is used only to upload videos made by this app to the channel of the account that gave permission.

The app does not read, collect, store or share your YouTube videos, comments, subscribers, contacts, email or any other personal data.

How data is stored

The access key needed for uploading is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository settings. It is never shown on this website and never shared with anyone.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties.

Removing access

You can remove this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos are uploaded under the YouTube Terms of Service.
